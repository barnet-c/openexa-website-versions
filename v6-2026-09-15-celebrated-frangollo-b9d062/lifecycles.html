In production / Lifecycle 01Financial markets / First application
Creation & redemption, briefly

An exchange-traded fund (ETF) has a market price and a net asset value (NAV): the value of its underlying holdings per share. Authorized participants create ETF shares by delivering a basket, or redeem shares for the basket. A premium or discount to NAV can create an opportunity, but execution cost, liquidity, borrow and risk determine whether it is actionable.

Inside the seven-step lifecycle

  1. Signal

    Data agents analyze price, NAV, basket composition and venue depth.

  2. Predict

    AI and machine-learning models score gap persistence and the risk of acting.

  3. Decide

    Strategy agents propose creation or redemption, size and timing under portfolio constraints.

  4. Approve

    The Execution Council applies policy. Human approval follows the configured operating mode; rejected proposals are logged too.

  5. Execute

    Approved actions pass through routing and deterministic execution, using broker, exchange, over-the-counter and clearing connections.

  6. Settle

    Clearing and settlement are tracked to completion, with exceptions surfaced for resolution.

  7. Reconcile

    Fills are matched to broker records and state transitions are hash-chained into the ledger.

    03 / Roadmap possibilities

    Follow the pattern.
    Not the industry.

    The architecture is designed to extend beyond its first use case. These are lifecycle possibilities, not claims of production deployments.

    Financial markets & insurance

    Possible extensions include basis, collateral, margin and insurance settlement workflows, as well as cross-exchange and pairs strategies. These are distinct from the fund creation and redemption lifecycle operating today.

    Relevant parties include brokers, authorized participants, custodians, clearing organizations and exchanges. Each extension would need its own policy, risk and execution evaluation.

    International trade / Letters of credit

    Potential scope: issuance, document presentation and checking, discrepancy handling, bank-to-bank confirmation and payment. The handoffs span issuing and advising banks, exporters, shippers, insurers and customs.

    Asset securitization / MBS & ABS

    Potential scope: pool assembly, eligibility and tranche compliance, servicer reporting, waterfall calculations and distributions for mortgage-backed and asset-backed securities. Relevant parties include originators, servicers, trustees, rating agencies and investors.

    Wholesale energy settlement

    Potential scope: scheduling, metering reconciliation, imbalance charges and multi-party netting. Coordination would span system operators, generators, traders, utilities and regulators.

    Semiconductor manufacturing

    Potential scope: order-to-wafer orchestration, yield exceptions, quality-gated release and export-control checks. The lifecycle would connect fabless designers, foundries, outsourced assembly and test providers, logistics and regulators.

    Pharmaceutical & aerospace

    Potential scope: regulated qualification, batch release, traceability and multi-supplier approval chains. Manufacturers, suppliers, regulators, operators and auditors would each retain their own responsibilities and controls.
