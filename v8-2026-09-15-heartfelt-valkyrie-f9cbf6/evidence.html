Which supporting records can be requested?

OpenEXA identifies TradeStation and Interactive Brokers confirmations, agent ledger entries and reconciliation reports as available for review on request. The underlying documents are not published on this page; no independent audit or certification is asserted.

About the 90% operating-model estimate

The supplied OpenEXA operating model presents an approximate 90% reduction in combined cost and risk using illustrative weights. This is an estimate, not an independently verified result or a universal reduction in risk.

The example assigns 45% to cost of carry, 37% to counterparty risk, 10% to technology and 8% to management. It assumes no carry or counterparty contribution, with technology reduced by 67% and management by 50%. These are modelling assumptions, not assurances that exposure disappears.

The comparison blends costs and risk rather than reporting a measured financial outcome. Ask for the methodology and deployment scope before using the estimate. Operational, counterparty and market risks remain.

Reported performance, execution records and operating-model estimates answer different questions. They are not interchangeable evidence.
