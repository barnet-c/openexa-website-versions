02The lifecycle testSix traits · all of them

The right work for a swarm.

Six traits help identify processes where coordinated, domain-specific agents have a clear job to do.

Multi-party

Many participants. A sequence that needs coordination.

Regulated

Rules that belong in the workflow itself.

Structured

Data and tools with defined formats.

Exception-heavy

Cases that need a specialist, not another handoff.

Gated

Actions that require explicit approval.

Audit-bound
