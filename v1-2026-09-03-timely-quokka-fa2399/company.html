Company

We build the agents. We know the lifecycles inside and out.

OpenEXA is an AI agentic company in Seattle. We build the end-to-end infrastructure on which swarms of domain-specific AI agents run high-value lifecycles — starting in the financial markets our team has spent careers inside.

01MissionAgents for the world's high-value lifecycles

OpenEXA is building the infrastructure for agentic lifecycles — where swarms of AI agents run the multi-party, regulated work that today runs on people, by exception.

The most valuable processes in the economy — settlement, creation and redemption, letters of credit, securitization, batch release — share six traits: many parties, a regulator, structured data, exceptions, approvals and an audit trail. Those traits make them expensive for humans and exactly right for narrow, verifiable agents with a shared ledger.

Our agents perceive, decide, act and prove at a scale and cost impossible for human-managed workflows — with an execution council, scoped permissions and an append-only ledger governing every one of them. We started in finance because the proof is measurable in basis points.

02The storyIdea · formation · thought leadership
  1. S-01

    The idea

    Combine deep experience from Big Tech and Wall Street with the latest advances in AI and autonomous agents to build the foundational infrastructure for agentic lifecycles — beginning with autonomous finance, where every result can be verified by a third party.

  2. S-02

    The formation

    To strengthen the foundation in quantitative finance and market expertise, Ajit and the team partnered with Dr. Tim Leung — Professor of Applied Mathematics and Director of Computational Finance and Risk Management at the University of Washington — and Barnet Sherman, a seasoned Wall Street trader and portfolio manager.

  3. S-03

    Thought leadership

    Research and collaboration partner with the University of Washington's Computational Finance and Risk Management program, including guest lectures and joint initiatives on FinTech, AI and blockchain.

  4. S-04

    Proof

    Lifecycle 01 — ETF creation and redemption — run by the swarm on live capital on NASDAQ and NYSE, verified by executing brokers, with three beta customers and $5M in committed assets.

  5. S-05

    Next

    The master–copy agent model across assets and strategies; then the marketplace; then the next lifecycle classes — trade finance, securitization, energy settlement, semiconductors, pharma and aerospace — on the same eight layers.
