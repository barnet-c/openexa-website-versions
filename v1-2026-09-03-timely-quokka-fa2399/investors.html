06The honest pageWhat you need to believe · reasons not to invest

What you need to believe

  1. AI agents can safely run a complex, regulated lifecycle end to end.

    Our answer is the proof page: ten sessions, both regimes, broker-verified, with custody untouched and every decision on the ledger.

  2. The 4–8% band is massive but structurally unprofitable for legacy Wall Street.

    At $0.0002 per dollar per day, no human-run desk can carry the cost of carry, counterparty risk and operations. The band is uncontested for a reason.

  3. Trust in autonomous agents is earned in the lowest-risk lifecycle first — then it compounds.

    The roadmap is sequenced from a single agent to multi-agent to a marketplace, and from finance to five more lifecycle classes.

Reasons not to invest

  1. You don't believe institutions will hand a lifecycle to an autonomous agent.

    They don't have to hand it anywhere: custody stays at their broker, and they choose the operating mode. Three beta customers already have.

  2. You're worried about flash-crash tail risks.

    So are we. Position caps, market-hours gates, deterministic limits and a database-backed halt exist for exactly that day — and they reduce, not remove, the risk.

  3. You think the largest desks will just build this internally.

    Perhaps for themselves. The opportunity is infrastructure that lets every other institution run its lifecycles with agents — a platform, not a desk.
