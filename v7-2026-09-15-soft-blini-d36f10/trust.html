01 / Control plane

Authority is granted.
Not assumed.

Permissions and approval are separate decisions: access to a system does not authorize every action inside it. The Council evaluates the proposal before execution.

01 / Scope

Permission scope

Allow only the resources, actions and limits a lifecycle needs. Revoke agent access when the mandate changes.

02 / Approval

An execution gate

Agents propose. The Execution Council checks policy, limits and conflicts before approval. Execution controls live in code, not only in a prompt.

03 / Oversight

Human operating modes

Use automatic, approval-required or manual operation for each lifecycle. Match oversight to the work, rather than treating autonomy as all-or-nothing.

Specific controls in Lifecycle 01
  • Allowed instruments and routing venues per lifecycle.
  • Position limits per instrument and pool; notional caps per action and session.
  • Market-hours windows and Council approval gates.
  • Scoped broker APIs without withdrawal permission; assets remain with the broker.
  • Execution-layer idempotency to prevent duplicate submissions, with reconciliation against broker records.
  • Database-backed emergency halt, retries with exponential backoff, and circuit breakers for disruptions.
How the three operating modes differ
  • Automatic. The Council evaluates proposals against policy without routine human sign-off.
  • Auto-with-approval. A human approval step sits between proposal review and execution.
  • Manual. The operator directs actions; scoped permissions and execution checks still apply.

02 / Records & intervention

Keep the trail.
Keep the stop.

Operators need to see what happened and intervene when conditions change. Records and runtime controls serve different purposes; neither replaces the other.

Tamper-evident records

Append-only, hash-chained entries connect proposals, approvals, submissions and outcomes—including rejected actions. Hash links help reveal changes; they do not make storage impossible to alter.

An operating stop

A database-backed halt is available in every mode. Stopping new activity is not the same as undoing work already accepted by another system.

Try the editable SHA-256 study
What the controls do not guarantee

Security and permission controls govern authorized activity; they do not eliminate model mistakes, compromised credentials, software defects or service outages. Hash verification depends on which records and reference hashes can be trusted.

For market-facing lifecycles, operational controls are separate from investment risk. Prices, liquidity, counterparties and settlement can move against a strategy. Broker custody does not make execution risk-free, and a halt cannot guarantee an immediate exit from existing exposure.

Operational controls are not investment protection. For market-facing lifecycles, capital remains at risk.
